A leather journal for your travels. Mark the places you find, sketch what you see, and tape in what you pick up along the way. The more you use it, the more worn it gets.

## Every blank page asks what's next

- **Mark this place.** The journal draws a map of the land around you, with a red X on the spot and a small arrow that always points the way back.
- **Sketch this view.** Hold it up, click, and it sketches what is in front of you in ink, mobs and all.
- **Draw something.** A small canvas, with the 16 dyes as your inks.
- **Write a note.** Your writing drifts a little on the page, like real handwriting.
- **Paste something in.** Tape in a flower from your first night out. Peel it off later and you get it back, just as it was.

## A book that keeps itself in order

The first page is a contents page that writes itself. Another lists every place you have been, and how far away it is. Paper tabs down the side and a red ribbon take you straight to what you are after.

## It wears in

Corners rub, pages fold, and a letter or the edge of an old map starts to poke out. A journal goes from New to Battered over five stages, and it never looks new again.

## Made for multiplayer

The pages live on the server. Anyone holding a journal can read it, but only its owner can write in it. Lose yours, and whoever finds it can read about your travels.

**Works with** [Exposure](https://modrinth.com/mod/exposure) on 1.21.1, so your photos can be taped in too.

**Requires** [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api). For Minecraft 1.20.1 and 1.21.1.

**Getting started:** craft the journal from a Book and Quill and a Compass, then right-click to open it.
