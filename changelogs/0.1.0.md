## Explorer's Journal 0.1.0

The first release. A leather journal you fill with maps, sketches, drawings, notes and keepsakes from your travels, and it wears in as it fills.

### What is in it

- **Mark a place.** A hand-drawn map of the land around you, with a red X on your spot and an arrow pointing the way back.
- **Sketch the view.** The journal draws the blocks and mobs in front of you as ink lines.
- **Draw by hand.** A small canvas with the 16 dyes, a fill bucket, straight lines and undo.
- **Write notes.** Handwriting that drifts a little, notes that run on to the next page, and `see:` links between entries.
- **Tape things in.** Items and Exposure photos, with a short note beside each one. Peel an item off and you get the same item back.
- **A contents page and a place list** that write themselves, with tabs and ribbons to get around.
- **5 stages of wear.** Rubbed corners, folded pages, and things poking out as the book fills.
- **Location Tooltip link.** Place names, names on the map, a pinned place on your screen with a compass, and naming villages from the journal.
- **Settings and commands** for server owners: private journals, paper for tape, the most entries per journal, and `/journal` to list or remove entries.

### Notes

- Each Minecraft version is a separate download. Pick the file that matches your game.
- The link with Location Tooltip needs Location Tooltip 0.8.0 or newer.
- Pages live in the world folder, under `data/explorers_journal`.

---
*Requires Fabric API. Location Tooltip and Exposure are optional.*
